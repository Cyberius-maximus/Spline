2. What Spline is — and is not

Spline is an informational fitness and wellbeing tool. It is not a medical device under Regulation (EU) 2017/745, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Biological-age estimates, readiness scores and laboratory interpretations are statistical surrogates with wide individual uncertainty, derived from published research and clearly cited in the app. They are not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a physician before acting on anything you read in Spline, and never delay seeking medical care because of it.

3. Free version and Spline Pro

The core of Spline is free and stays free: daily readiness and its drivers, the HRV gate, sleep, recovery, training load, the workout and strength logs, the food log, the starting training plan, widgets and the Apple Watch app, every citation and formula, and full export of your own data as JSON or CSV.

Spline Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription which additionally unlocks:

  • Biological age — the wearable estimate of the age your vitals read as, with the years each factor adds or subtracts
  • Bloodwork and lab import — lab PDF and photo import, PhenoAge, ApoB and Lp(a) analysis, change significance tested against the Reference Change Value, and the peptide effect tracker
  • Experiments and correlations — the N-of-1 engine with effect sizes, bootstrap confidence intervals and false-discovery-rate correction, and automatically found habit patterns
  • Adaptive planning — race-aware periodization and strength progression targets
  • The monthly review
  • Ask Spline and shortcuts — answers to questions about your own data, and typed sentences turned into logged sets or a routine

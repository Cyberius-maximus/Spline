Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · This is the Privacy Policy URL for Tilde’s App Store listing.

The short version

Tilde has no servers and makes no internet connections of its own. What you log is stored on your iPhone and analysed on your iPhone. If you use Tilde’s widgets or its Apple Watch app, a short summary also sits on those — still only on your own devices. There is no account, no sign-up, no cloud sync operated by us, and no analytics or advertising SDK in the app. We cannot see your data, because it never reaches us.

Data the app processes on your device

  • What you log: period days and flow, spotting, pain, mood, energy, symptoms (including ones you name yourself) and notes.
  • Your settings: typical cycle and period length, the mode you chose (natural cycles, trying to conceive, perimenopause or hormonal contraception), and, if you enter it, your birth year, used only to recognise perimenopause patterns.
  • Apple Health (HealthKit): if you turn it on, Tilde reads menstrual flow, spotting, sleeping wrist temperature, heart-rate variability and resting heart rate, and the symptom types listed below, each only after you grant permission for that type. It writes the flow, spotting and symptoms you log (such as cramps, headache, bloating, breast pain, acne, lower back pain, fatigue, appetite changes, nausea, sleep changes, mood changes and hot flushes), so your other health apps can account for your cycle.
  • Reminders: notifications you turn on are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Widgets: to show today on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and in StandBy, Tilde keeps a short summary — your mode, cycle day, today’s phase, the next-period window and how sure that estimate is, plus what you logged today — in storage that only Tilde and its own widgets on your iPhone can read. Widgets never see your past logs. The “Log today” button in Control Center only opens the app.
  • Apple Watch: if you install the watch app, your iPhone sends the same summary to your paired Apple Watch over the encrypted connection Apple provides between an iPhone and its watch, and the watch keeps the latest one for the app and its complications. What you log on the watch (flow, pain and symptoms) goes straight back to your iPhone and is stored there. Tilde has no server along the way, and we never see it.

Everything you log is kept in one file in the app’s own storage on your iPhone, with iOS’s complete file protection: it is encrypted and unreadable while your iPhone is locked. In line with Apple’s HealthKit requirements, health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold or shared with data brokers.

When data does leave the app

Only in these cases, and only because you chose them:

  • Apple Health: what Tilde writes there is governed by your Health settings, including whether Apple syncs Health data through your iCloud account.
  • Exports: exporting creates a JSON file of all your data. Where it goes — Files, AirDrop, your own iCloud Drive — is entirely your choice.
  • The cycle summary: if you share the report for your doctor, it goes through the iOS share sheet to wherever you choose.
  • Sharing with Spline (off by default): if you also use Spline and turn on “Share cycle phase with Spline”, Tilde keeps a small summary (your mode, today’s phase and cycle day, the next-period window, whether a temperature rise is expected, and the confidence) in storage that only apps from the same developer can read on the same iPhone. It never leaves your device, and it is separate from the summary Tilde’s own widgets use, which Spline cannot read. Turning the setting off or deleting your data removes it.
  • Device backups: iOS includes app data in the iCloud or computer backups you have set up for your iPhone.

Children

Tilde is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly process their data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to processing of your personal data. Because your data lives only on your device, you exercise most of these directly: export it any time as JSON, or delete every log in Settings under Your data, which also clears the summary on your widgets and your Apple Watch. Data already saved to Apple Health stays there until you remove it in the Health app. For anything else, contact us below. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

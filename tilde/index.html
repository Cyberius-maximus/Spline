Why a tilde

The name is the product, twice over.

~

A wave

The tilde is a small sine curve: the monthly rise and fall of oestradiol, progesterone, temperature and heart-rate variability.

≈

About, not exactly

In maths, ~ means approximately. Every Tilde prediction is a range with its confidence, and the range widens on its own when your cycles vary.

How it works

Everything other cycle apps do, minus the guesswork they present as fact.

Ranges, not dates

Your period and fertile window come as ranges with a confidence label. The fertile window is drawn as a probability band, not a block of “red days”.

Three taps a day

One sheet for flow, pain from 0 to 10, mood, energy and symptoms, plus symptoms you name yourself.

Your patterns, free

Cycle lengths against the typical 22–36-day band, a symptom heat map by cycle day, and your own premenstrual window.

Worth a conversation

Calm flags with the threshold and source shown: unusual cycle lengths, 60 days without a period, heavy or long bleeding, severe pain, recurring severe low mood before a period.

A mode for where you are

Natural cycles, trying to conceive, perimenopause (using the STRAW+10 bleeding criteria), and hormonal contraception, where Tilde never invents phases you don’t have.

Any wearable

Reads wrist temperature, HRV and resting heart rate from Apple Health, whichever device recorded them, and compares them with your own phase baseline.

Notifications, one by one

Period likely soon, a daily log reminder, fertile window opening, a premenstrual heads-up. Turn each one on or off separately.

A report for your doctor

A plain summary of your recent cycles that you can share from your iPhone before an appointment.

Part of Apple Health

The flow and symptoms you log are written to Apple Health, so your other health apps can take your cycle into account.

Evidence on every point

Each piece of guidance opens into a short article: what’s going on, what the research found, what to try, where the evidence stops, and the studies. Some examples from the app:

Strong

Iron still matters

Heavy periods are a common, overlooked cause of iron deficiency. Around 38% of women of reproductive age are iron-deficient without anaemia; a ferritin under 30 ng/mL is diagnostic.

Auerbach 2025, JAMA
Moderate

Hungrier? That’s expected

People eat about 168 kcal a day more after ovulation. It’s a real signal, not a lapse.

Tucker 2025, Nutrition Reviews
Limited

Warm up properly

Injury-prevention warm-ups cut ACL injuries by 38% in trials of female athletes. The idea that a late-cycle hormone raises the risk is plausible but unproven, so Tilde says so.

Gu 2026, Frontiers in Public Health
✕ Myth

“Ovulation is on day 14”

The fertile window can fall anywhere from day 6 to day 21, and only about 30% of women have it entirely within days 10–17.

Wilcox 2000, BMJ
✕ Myth

“Sync your workouts to your phase”

On average, performance differs only trivially across the cycle, and strength and muscle gains don’t depend on phase. Train to your plan; your own logs will show if you’re the exception.

McNulty 2020, Sports Medicine; Colenso-Semple 2023, Frontiers
✕ Myth

“Seed cycling balances your hormones”

Only small, uncontrolled studies exist. Tilde keeps a whole section of myths it won’t sell you.

Nagarajan 2025, Cureus

Questions

Is Tilde contraception?

No. Tilde estimates, and it never marks a day as “safe”. If you need contraception, talk to your doctor about a method made for it.

My cycles are irregular. Will the predictions work?

Tilde starts from typical lengths and switches to your own numbers once it has three of your cycles. When your cycles vary, the ranges widen and the confidence drops, so you always see how sure the estimate is.

I’m on the pill or another hormonal method.

Choose the hormonal contraception mode. Tilde then tracks bleeding and symptoms without inventing phases you don’t have, and explains what the evidence says about training and symptoms on hormonal contraception.

Which devices does it work with?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Temperature, HRV and resting heart rate come from Apple Health, so any watch or ring that writes them there works. You can also use Tilde with no wearable at all.

How do I export or delete my data?

In Settings, under Your data: export everything as a JSON file, or delete every log from your iPhone. Anything already saved to Apple Health stays there until you remove it in the Health app.

Do I need Spline to use Tilde?

No. Tilde and Spline are separate apps from the same maker. Each works on its own.

Is Tilde available in German?

Not yet. Tilde starts in English.

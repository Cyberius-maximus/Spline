About, not exactly

The app

Everything a cycle app does, minus the guesswork it sells as fact.

Three taps a day.

One sheet for the whole day. Pick a flow, slide the pain, tap a mood and the symptoms you have. Name your own if they aren’t there.

  • Medium flow
  • Pain 0–10
  • Mood
  • Energy
  • Cramps
  • Bloating
  • + your own

Predictions you can see through.

Logged days are filled in. What Tilde expects is dashed, and the possible fertile days are ringed, so you never mistake a forecast for a fact.

  • Your own numbers after three cycles; typical lengths only until then.
  • Wider when you vary. Irregular cycles get wider ranges, not false precision.
  • A confidence label on every forecast, high, medium or low.

Your patterns, free.

Six cycles in, Tilde shows what’s normal for you: your cycle length and its spread against the typical 22–36 days, and a heat map of which symptoms land on which cycle days.

  • Your personal pre-period window, found in your own logs.
  • No paywall on your own history.

Worth a conversation

Calm flags with the threshold and source shown: unusual cycle lengths, 60 days without a period, heavy or long bleeding, severe pain, recurring severe low mood before a period.

A mode for where you are

Natural cycles, trying to conceive, perimenopause (using the STRAW+10 bleeding criteria), and hormonal contraception, where Tilde never invents phases you don’t have.

Any wearable

Reads wrist temperature, HRV and resting heart rate from Apple Health, whichever device recorded them, and compares them with your own phase baseline.

Notifications, one by one

Period likely soon, a daily log reminder, fertile window opening, a premenstrual heads-up. Turn each one on or off on its own.

A report for your doctor

A plain summary of your recent cycles that you can share from your iPhone before an appointment.

Part of Apple Health

The flow and symptoms you log are written to Apple Health, so your other health apps can take your cycle into account.

Myths we don’t sell

Other cycle apps build features on these. Tilde files them under ✕ Not supported.

  • “Sync your workouts to your phase”

    The research found differences too small to build a training plan on.McNulty 2020, Sports Medicine

  • “Ovulation is on day 14”

    Only about 30% of women have their whole fertile window inside days 10–17.Wilcox 2000, BMJ

  • “Seed cycling balances your hormones”

    Seeds are good food; timing them to your phases has never been properly tested.Nagarajan 2025, Cureus

  • “On the pill you still have phases”

    Hormonal contraception holds your own hormones steady. There’s nothing to sync to.Elliott-Sale 2020, Sports Medicine

  • “Losing your period means you’re training hard”

    It’s a warning sign of too little energy for your training, not a badge.Mountjoy 2023, BJSM

Questions

Is Tilde contraception?

No. Tilde estimates, and it never marks a day as “safe”. If you need contraception, talk to your doctor about a method made for it.

My cycles are irregular. Will the predictions work?

Tilde starts from typical lengths and switches to your own numbers once it has three of your cycles. When your cycles vary, the ranges widen and the confidence drops, so you always see how sure the estimate is.

I’m on the pill or another hormonal method.

Choose the hormonal contraception mode. Tilde then tracks bleeding and symptoms without inventing phases you don’t have, and explains what the evidence says about training and symptoms on hormonal contraception.

Which devices does it work with?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, with widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen. The optional Apple Watch app (watchOS 10 or later) shows today’s day and phase and lets you log flow, pain and symptoms from your wrist. Temperature, HRV and resting heart rate come from Apple Health, so any watch or ring that writes them there works. You can also use Tilde with no wearable at all.

How do I export or delete my data?

In Settings, under Your data: export everything as a JSON file, or delete every log from your iPhone. Anything already saved to Apple Health stays there until you remove it in the Health app.

Do I need Spline to use Tilde?

No. Tilde and Spline are separate apps from the same maker. Each works on its own.

Is Tilde available in German?

Not yet. Tilde starts in English.
